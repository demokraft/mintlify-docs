## One Piece of Knowledge, Many Assets

A single product walkthrough doesn't have to stay a single asset. The same recording can become a polished video, a how-to guide, a help document, a GIF, and localized versions — serving marketing, sales, support, and training from one source.

  </div>
</Frame>

## How Much Should You Create This Way?

<Tip>
  Flagship launch films and major brand campaigns still benefit from specialist creative agencies. Most of what organizations need day to day — explainers, demos, updates, training, support content — is about speed and scale, not bespoke production.
</Tip>

Look at what you already create: launch videos, explainers, demos, tutorials, training, help content, partner enablement, technical guides. Now imagine producing it in minutes instead of weeks — and turning knowledge you already have into content for more people, in more formats and languages.
