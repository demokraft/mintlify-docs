## Brand Kit overview

Brand Kit is an AI feature in Demokraft for managing reusable brand details across your videos.

<Frame>
  <img src="https://storage.googleapis.com/dk-transcoder-assets/seller/dca5540c-3f1e-494a-950f-ef227365a8d7/studio/820aaf46-e7dd-4ae7-9abc-298ad7cd0149/video_screenshot_file/docv2_6.jpg" alt="Enter the website that represents your brand, then select Detect" />
</Frame>

It lets you preconfigure those details and apply them consistently to multiple videos, helping them comply with your brand guidelines.

Instead of entering details for every project, attach an existing Brand Kit during video creation. It can include brand colors, website, fonts, logo, product name, tagline, call to action, and other brand guidelines.

You can also use Auto Detect from Website to detect brand guidelines from your website, then review and refine them. Demokraft supports up to 10 Brand Kits for different video needs.

## Create and configure a Brand Kit

A Brand Kit identifies brand details and guidelines for videos. Use these steps to create one, review the detected details, set the brand voice, and save the configuration.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Open **Settings** to view and manage existing Brand Kits.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://storage.googleapis.com/dk-transcoder-assets/seller/dca5540c-3f1e-494a-950f-ef227365a8d7/studio/820aaf46-e7dd-4ae7-9abc-298ad7cd0149/video_screenshot_file/docv2_5.jpg" alt="Open Settings to view and manage existing Brand Kits" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add a brand kit">
    Select **Add brand kit** to create a Brand Kit.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure brand details">
    Enter the website that represents your brand, then select **Detect** (optional if you want to auto detect from a website, else you can fill manually).

    - Review and update the company name, product name, tagline, brand colors, fonts, logo, call to action, and favicon.
    - Add both dark and light logo versions, along with your favicon, for complete coverage.
    - Set the tone and speaking pace for your videos.
    - Update any fields as needed, including fields filled by automatic detection.
    - Select **Save** when the configuration is complete. You can associate the saved Brand Kit with videos you create.
    - You can **add/update/delete** brand kits in this settings section.
    - You can create up to 10 brand kits as per your needs.

## Apply a Brand Kit to videos

Brand Kits work with **Prompt a Studio**, **Prompt a Motion**, and **Prompt a Motion Premium**. You select the kit the same way in all three.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a prompt feature">
    From the home page, open **Prompt a Studio**, **Prompt a Motion**, or **Prompt a Motion Premium**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://storage.googleapis.com/dk-transcoder-assets/seller/dca5540c-3f1e-494a-950f-ef227365a8d7/studio/820aaf46-e7dd-4ae7-9abc-298ad7cd0149/video_screenshot_file/docv2_10.jpg" alt="Open Prompt Premium Video Creation" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set Brand Kit in website tab">
    In the website tab, set a Brand Kit (if you had a default kit it would be already set), or choose **Auto Detect from Website** for the URL you provide.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select Brand Kit in prompt tab">
    In the prompt tab, under **Color Palette** select the Brand Kit. The **Font** picker switches to the kit's font.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://storage.googleapis.com/dk-transcoder-assets/seller/dca5540c-3f1e-494a-950f-ef227365a8d7/studio/820aaf46-e7dd-4ae7-9abc-298ad7cd0149/video_screenshot_file/docv2_12.jpg" alt="Under Color Palette in the prompt settings, select the Brand Kit" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once the brand kit is selected, it will generate based on the selected brand kit's guidelines automatically.

### How a Brand Kit is applied

- **Website tab:** the kit's details replace the ones detected from the website. Any field you left empty in the kit is taken from the website. If the kit belongs to a different website than the one you entered, a warning appears so you can switch to the site's own brand.
- **Prompt tab:** colors or a font you write in the prompt take priority over the kit. The kit's name, tagline, call to action, and website are used exactly as saved.
- **Prompt a Studio:** the **Templates** panel theme also starts with the kit's colors and font. You can change it in the editor.
- The kit is read when generation starts. Changes you make to a kit later apply to new videos, not to ones already created.

<Note>
  Do not select a brand kit for a video if you want to generate a video with a different theme.
</Note>
