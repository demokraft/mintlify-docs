## Prompt a Motion overview

Prompt a Motion is Demokraft AI’s feature for quickly creating professional, brand-aligned motion-graphics videos from a website URL or product prompt.

Prompt a Studio generates a fully editable Studio project, giving you hands-on control over the storyboard, outline, scenes, layouts, and narration suggestions.

By contrast, Prompt a Motion uses a more streamlined approach without building an editable project from scratch.

You set the video’s purpose and duration, review and refine the outline and storyboard, and export the result, making Prompt a Motion suited to workflows where speed and simplicity matter more than extensive hands-on editing.

## Configure video generation

Configure Prompt a Motion from the portal, then complete the required video and audio settings before processing starts.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Prompt a Motion">
    Select **Prompt a Motion** from the available video creation options after signing in to the portal. A pop-up window opens with options similar to those in Prompt a Studio.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://storage.googleapis.com/dk-transcoder-assets/seller/dca5540c-3f1e-494a-950f-ef227365a8d7/studio/893d902d-2a74-43b6-9e9f-fe1442546287/video_screenshot_file/docv2_0.jpg" alt="Prompt a Motion selection modal" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure video options">
    Enter your website URL and specify the video's language, duration, and purpose. Add custom instructions, such as the target audience or other requirements, to guide the video generation.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://storage.googleapis.com/dk-transcoder-assets/seller/dca5540c-3f1e-494a-950f-ef227365a8d7/studio/893d902d-2a74-43b6-9e9f-fe1442546287/video_screenshot_file/docv2_1.jpg" alt="Configure language, duration, and purpose settings" />
    </Frame>

    <Tip>
      **Purpose** sets the story shape (Explainer, How-To, etc.) and is used even if you don't type anything about video type. Only your own custom instructions or prompt can override it — your website's text is never used to detect purpose.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Info>
  **Supported Languages (39 live, 2 coming soon)**

  * **English**
  * **Latin script:** French, Spanish, German, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Turkish, Polish, Swedish, Romanian, Czech, Croatian, Danish, Slovak, Finnish, Norwegian, Indonesian, Malay, Filipino, Vietnamese
  * **Cyrillic + Greek:** Russian, Ukrainian, Bulgarian, Greek
  * **Indic scripts:** Hindi, Marathi, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, Gujarati, Bengali, Punjabi, Assamese, Malayalam
  * **CJK + Thai:** Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Thai
  * **Coming soon:** Arabic, Urdu
  </Info>


  <Step title="Select voice and music">
    Preview the available voices and select the one that best fits your video. Choose a music track and adjust its volume to suit your preference. Complete these voice and music selections before starting generation; unlike Prompt a Studio, Prompt a Motion does not let you choose them later in the Studio video editor. When the audio selections are complete, click **Generate Video**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Use prompt inputs (optional)">
    If you want to enter a prompt, use the **Prompt tab** to enter your product, purpose, key requirements, and preferred images, following the sample template if helpful. Upload images and select the voice and music.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://storage.googleapis.com/dk-transcoder-assets/seller/dca5540c-3f1e-494a-950f-ef227365a8d7/studio/893d902d-2a74-43b6-9e9f-fe1442546287/video_screenshot_file/docv2_3.jpg" alt="Prompt tab options" />
    </Frame>

    <Tip>
      **Prompt tab only:** a **Palette** picker (presets: Cool, Warm, Vibrant, Monochrome, Dark, Earthy — or **Custom** colors) and a **Font** picker sit next to the image upload button. **Colors or a font stated in your prompt always take priority over these pickers.** Leave them as-is for the same default as before.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Generate Video">
    Click **Generate Video** to begin processing and create the initial outline.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Create and approve the outline

Prompt a Motion uses your product information and media to prepare an outline before creating the video.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Outline generation">
    Demokraft AI analyzes your inputs, drafts the story, and prepares the outline. Processing time depends on the amount of information, duration, and number of images provided.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review and approve">
    Review the outline, which shows the video's overall flow and scenes, including each scene's purpose and direction rather than detailed scene-level specifications. Add comments in the feedback section to request refinements, then click **Approve** when the outline meets your needs. The workflow continues only after approval.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://storage.googleapis.com/dk-transcoder-assets/seller/dca5540c-3f1e-494a-950f-ef227365a8d7/studio/893d902d-2a74-43b6-9e9f-fe1442546287/video_screenshot_file/docv2_4.jpg" alt="Review and approve outline" />
    </Frame>

    <Note>
      A **Video type** dropdown also appears here — it's usually blank, which is normal and doesn't mean your Purpose choice was ignored. Only use it to correct or narrow the type further.
    </Note>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Review the storyboard

Review the generated storyboard details, request revisions when needed, and approve the storyboard when it is ready.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Inspect storyboard content">
    The storyboard shows the color theme, aesthetic directions, images that will be used, and scene-level details.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review details and request revisions">
    Review the storyboard details, including colors, visual style, tone, images, script, rough layout, voice-over, and duration. Leave feedback to request revisions if necessary.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://storage.googleapis.com/dk-transcoder-assets/seller/dca5540c-3f1e-494a-950f-ef227365a8d7/studio/893d902d-2a74-43b6-9e9f-fe1442546287/video_screenshot_file/docv2_6.jpg" alt="Storyboard details review" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Approve storyboard">
    Click **Approve** when the storyboard is ready. Demokraft AI begins building the video.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://storage.googleapis.com/dk-transcoder-assets/seller/dca5540c-3f1e-494a-950f-ef227365a8d7/studio/893d902d-2a74-43b6-9e9f-fe1442546287/video_screenshot_file/docv2_7.jpg" alt="Approve storyboard step" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Each of the steps takes time based on the information provided, images, and duration. It might take a few minutes.
</Note>

## Review and export the video

Wait for Demokraft AI to analyze your inputs, generate the final video, and open it in the Studio video editor once built. This processing may take some time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review video">
    Review the complete video by moving through each scene and using play and pause to check the result. Add captions if needed; no other editing options are available.

    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Export video">
    Click **Export** and select the resolution you need. The finished video is ready to save at the selected resolution.
