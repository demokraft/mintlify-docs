## 1. Which option should I use?

Both options sit on the Demokraft AI Portal Home page and take exactly the same two kinds of input[cite: 2]. What differs is what gets built[cite: 2].

| Feature | **Prompt a Studio** | **Prompt a Motion** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **What you get** | A scene-based product video assembled from DemoKraft's scene templates—your screenshots, logo, and photos placed into layouts[cite: 2]. | A **motion-graphics** video—animated headlines, counters, icon flows, abstract product-UI cards, all moving smoothly[cite: 2]. |
| **Best for** | Custom, reusable, highly controlled content[cite: 2] | Fast, polished video creation[cite: 2] |
| **Creative control** | High[cite: 2] | Focused on outline/storyboard approval[cite: 2] |
| **Editing** | Fully editable Studio project[cite: 2] | Designed for fast generation[cite: 2] |
| **Voice** | Scripts are written for you; **you generate the voice yourself** in the editor[cite: 2]. | **Narration is generated during the build** with the voice you pick before hitting Generate[cite: 2]. |
| **Music** | You add it in the editor[cite: 2]. | You pick the track (or upload your own) before hitting Generate[cite: 2]. |
| **After it's built** | Fully editable in the Studio editor—scripts, effects, backgrounds, everything[cite: 2]. | Captions and Export stay open; changes are made by **typing feedback** in the editor's Feedback panel (3 rounds)[cite: 2]. |

<Tip>
  **Rule of thumb:** **Prompt a Motion** is ideal when speed and simplicity matter more than extensive hands-on editing[cite: 2]. **Prompt a Studio** is ideal when you need full creative control and an editable project[cite: 2].
</Tip>

## 2. Website tab or Prompt tab?

Both options give you the same two tabs[cite: 2]. Pick the one where your best information actually lives[cite: 2].

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Website Tab" icon="globe">
    **Use when:** Your product site is public, loads without a login, matches your intended brand theme, and clearly states what the product is, who it's for, and key benefits[cite: 2].
    
    *The app automatically extracts copy, headings, colors, fonts, and images from the page[cite: 2].*
  </Card>
  
  <Card title="Prompt Tab" icon="pen-to-square">
    **Use when:**
    - Product is unlaunched or behind a login/waitlist[cite: 2].
    - Site undersells the feature you want to feature[cite: 2].
    - Site is image-heavy with minimal text[cite: 2].
    - You want tight control over wording and emphasis[cite: 2].
    - Creating a specific feature, release, or concept video[cite: 2].
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  **Attaching Images:** You can attach images on either tab[cite: 2]. On the Website tab, attached images are used *alongside* scraped web assets—handy for uploading a high-res logo or unreleased UI screenshot[cite: 2].
</Note>

<Warning title="The Golden Rule of AI Inputs">
  **Nothing is invented[cite: 2].** The video will not claim a price, a guarantee, an integration, a customer count, or a capability you didn't state[cite: 2]. A thin input produces a shorter, plainer video—not a padded one[cite: 2]. If you want it on screen, say it[cite: 2].
</Warning>

## 3. The Nine Core Input Elements

Whichever tab you use, the system extracts these key elements[cite: 2]. Every item you supply is one less detail left blank[cite: 2].

| # | What to state | What it becomes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Product name** + one-line **tagline**[cite: 2] | Opening and closing scenes, spoken and on screen[cite: 2] |
| **2** | **What it actually does** (in plain words)[cite: 2] | The spine of the entire narrative[cite: 2] |
| **3** | **Who it's for**[cite: 2] | The vocabulary and tone of every line of narration[cite: 2] |
| **4** | **The purpose of *this* video**[cite: 2] | Which story gets told—an overview, a launch, or an intro[cite: 2] |
| **5** | **The problem** it removes[cite: 2] | The opening beat; without it, the video starts flat[cite: 2] |
| **6** | **3–5 benefits** (outcomes, not features)[cite: 2] | The middle scenes—roughly one core idea per scene[cite: 2] |
| **7** | **Proof: numbers, steps, quotes, integrations**[cite: 2] | Unlocks high-value scene types (see Section 5)[cite: 2] |
| **8** | **Call to Action (CTA)** + URL[cite: 2] | The closing scene[cite: 2] |
| **9** | **Brand colors and font** *(optional)*[cite: 2] | Essential for custom brand themes in the Prompt tab[cite: 2] |

<Info>
  **Image Labeling:** If you attach images, state what each file is—especially which file is your logo[cite: 2]!
</Info>

### Focus on Outcomes, Not Features

Write the business or user outcome rather than the technical mechanism[cite: 2]:

| ✅ Write This (Outcome) | ❌ Avoid This (Feature) |
| :--- | :--- |
| "Close the month in an afternoon instead of three days"[cite: 2] | "Automated reconciliation engine"[cite: 2] |
| "Never send a quote with stale pricing again"[cite: 2] | "Real-time pricing sync"[cite: 2] |
| "See which deals are slipping before your 1:1"[cite: 2] | "Predictive pipeline analytics"[cite: 2] |

## 4. Writing the Prompt

### The Skeleton Template

Copy this template, fill it in, and delete unused fields[cite: 2]. Category labels help the AI parse structure[cite: 2]. 

<Tip>
  Use the **Use sample** or **Copy sample** links above the prompt box in the portal for a pre-filled, editable version[cite: 2].
</Tip>

```text title="Prompt Skeleton Template"
Product: <name> — "<one-line tagline>"

What it is: <2-3 plain sentences. What it does, for whom, and how.>

Purpose of this video: <what THIS video should announce or focus on>

Target audience: <who's watching, and how they should be spoken to>

Tone and emphasis: <e.g., calm and precise; emphasize security over speed>

The problem: <what life looks like without this, concretely>

Key benefits: <3-5 outcomes, one per line or comma-separated>

Proof: <real numbers with units and labels; a customer quote; named integrations>

How it works: <2-4 ordered steps, if there's a clear sequence>

Call to action: <what you want viewers to do, plus the URL>

Brand guidelines: <primary color, accent color, background, font in HTML hex values>

Uploaded images: <filename> is our logo. <filename> is a product screenshot of home page. …

```

### Prompt Length Guidelines

* **Prompt Tab:** 40 characters minimum, 8,000 maximum (live counter active).


* **Website Tab (Custom Instructions):** 5,000 characters maximum.


* **Recommended Length:** **800–2,500 characters** (roughly the size of the template above). Shorter inputs result in generic middle scenes; excessively long inputs get trimmed for a 30-second video.

## 5. Earning High-Impact Scenes

Certain animated scene types are unlocked **only** when your input provides the required raw data.

| Target Scene Type | Required Input Material |
| --- | --- |
| **Number Count-Up (Stat Scene)**<br> | Explicit metric with units and context: *"39 languages supported"*, *"4x faster close"*, *"12,000 teams"*. *(A generic "very fast" will not trigger this)*.

 |
| **How-it-Works Flow**<br> | **2–4 ordered steps**: *"Connect billing tool → Map data → Forecast updates nightly."*<br> |
| **Before/After Split**<br> | Both states explicitly defined: *"Before: three days of spreadsheets. After: daily automated forecast."*<br> |
| **Social Proof / Quote**<br> | Exact testimonial quote and attribution.

 |
| **Ecosystem / Integrations**<br> | Specific tool names spelled out: *"Stripe, HubSpot, NetSuite."*<br> |
| **Logo Landing**<br> | Logo file attached and explicitly labeled in text. *(Fallback: product name wordmark)*.

 |
| **Product Screen Showcase**<br> | Attached clear screenshot or public site image.

 |
| **Strong Closing CTA**<br> | ~3-word CTA + URL: *"Start free trial — zephyr.io/trial"*.

 |

## 6. Image Guidelines

Images are optional on both tabs, but they are the single highest quality lever after prompt text.

1. **Limit & Formats:**
Upload up to **5 files** in `JPG`, `PNG`, `WEBP`, or `SVG` format. (SVG logos are automatically converted).


2. **Label Filenames in Text:**
Map filenames in your prompt:
`brandmark.svg is our logo. dashboard.png is the main dashboard UI. team-photo.jpg is a lifestyle photo.`


3. **Ensure Screenshot Quality:**
Use full-window, high-resolution screenshots with real data and clean UI. Blurry images are automatically skipped.


4. **People vs. Product UI:**
Founder headshots/avatars are automatically recognized and will not be forced into product frames.

## 7. Configuration Pickers

Located next to the **Generate Video** button:

### 1. Duration

Budget roughly 1 scene per 5 seconds (e.g., 30s ≈ 6 scenes).

| Content Volume | Recommended Duration |
| --- | --- |
| Single message, quick teaser, or feature launch

 | **15 – 30s**<br> |
| Problem statement + 3 benefits + CTA

 | **30 – 45s**<br> |
| Comprehensive story with proof and workflow

 | **60 – 90s**<br> |

### 2. Video Type

* **Intro:** Brand-first overview (Who we are, what we do, why it matters).


* **Explainer:** The core workhorse (Problem → Solution → How it works → Outcome).


* **Launch:** News-driven focus (What's new, why now, what it unlocks). Specify what feature is launching in your prompt!



### 3. Language

* **Current Support:** English only.


* **Upcoming:** Additional language support is coming soon.

## 10. Reviewing & Iterating Storyboards

You receive **two review checkpoints** before final video assembly. Use them to refine content without wasting generation credits.

```
Input Prompt ➔ [ Checkpoint A: Story Direction ] ➔ [ Checkpoint B: Full Storyboard ] ➔ Video Build

```

* **Checkpoint A (Story Direction):** Review scene count and 1-line scene summaries. Fix narrative focus here.


* **Checkpoint B (Full Storyboard):** Review exact layout, visuals, on-screen copy, and voiceover text.



### Effective Feedback Examples

| ✅ Specific & Actionable | ❌ Vague & Ineffective |
| --- | --- |
| "Scene 2 should lead with time savings, not integrations."

 | "Make it better."

 |
| "Cut Scene 4 and allocate time to the closing CTA."

 | "It feels too long."

 |
| "Use dashboard.png screenshot in Scene 3."

 | "Wrong images."

 |
| "Replace 'revolutionary' with 'built for finance teams'."

 | "Tone is off."

## 13. Pre-Flight Checklist

Before clicking **Generate Video**, verify your input:

Product name and 1-line tagline included
Concrete problem statement clearly defined
3–5 key benefits expressed as outcomes
At least one proof point added (number, steps, quote, or integrations)
Video purpose specified (especially for Launch videos)
Target audience defined
3-word CTA + URL included
Brand hex colors and font specified (if custom theme desired)
Images attached and labeled by filename (logo specified)
Duration selected matching content volume
*(Prompt a Motion)* Voice, speed, and music selected
