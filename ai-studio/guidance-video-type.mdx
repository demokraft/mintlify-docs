Demokraft AI makes **product and solution videos**: videos that show your product, explain what it does, and teach people how to use it. You start from something you already have (a screen recording, a slide deck, or your product website URL) or from a description of your product, and Demokraft AI builds the video for you.

It is built for software, SaaS, and technology companies, whether your product is a web app or desktop software. It is used by founders and leaders, product management, product marketing, sales, customer success, and learning and development (L&D) teams. Use it to help customers understand and adopt your product, and to help your sales team close deals.

Read this page before you create your first project. It takes two minutes and helps you pick the right starting point.

## What Demokraft AI is built for

Every video is about your product. It is built from your screen recording or slides, or from your website and product description, with animated text, shapes, and motion graphics in your brand colors. AI helps with the script, the voiceover, captions, and music.

| What you want to make | Best way to start |
|---|---|
| **SaaS launch, intro, and explainer videos** | [Prompt a Motion Premium](/ai-studio/pamp), [Prompt a Motion](/ai-studio/prompt-a-motion), [Prompt a Studio](/ai-studio/prompt-a-studio), or a [template](/ai-studio/how-templates) |
| **Feature introductions and new feature release announcements** | Any of the prompt options, or a template |
| **SaaS product walkthroughs and software application demos** | [Record your screen](/ai-studio/screen-rec) or upload a screen recording |
| **Step-by-step product demonstrations** | Record or upload a screen recording |
| **Product setup and configuration guides** | Record or upload a screen recording |
| **Dashboard and functionality demonstrations** | Record or upload a screen recording |
| **Before-and-after workflow comparisons** | Any of the prompt options: describe both the old way and the new way |
| **Customer education, support, and training videos** | Record or upload a screen recording, or [convert a presentation](/ai-studio/presentation) |
| **Help doc guides** | [Generate a doc](/ai-studio/generate-doc) from a recorded, uploaded, or presentation project |
| **Animated GIFs for your website, emails, and docs** | [Export](/ai-studio/export) a finished video as a GIF |
| **Short clips for social media** | [Prompt a Motion Premium](/ai-studio/pamp) in portrait or square format |

## When to use which option

Every option is on the **Home** page. Pick the one that matches what you have and what you need. The three prompt options start from your website URL or a product description. Product screenshots are nice to have, not required.

| Option | Use it when | Example |
|---|---|---|
| [Record your screen](/ai-studio/screen-rec) | You want to show your real product, step by step | "How to invite a teammate to your workspace" |
| **Upload a screen recording** | You already have a recording from another tool | Turn a raw demo recording into a polished, narrated tutorial |
| [Convert a presentation](/ai-studio/presentation) | Your content is already in a slide deck | Turn your quarterly product update deck into a narrated video |
| [Prompt a Motion Premium](/ai-studio/pamp) | You want the most polished animated look, or a portrait or square video for social media | A 30-second portrait launch clip for LinkedIn or Instagram |
| [Prompt a Motion](/ai-studio/prompt-a-motion) | You want an animated video fast, with little editing | A 30-second animated teaser for a new feature |
| [Prompt a Studio](/ai-studio/prompt-a-studio) | You have no recording, and you want a video you can fully edit | A 60-second explainer of your invoicing feature |
| [Start from a template](/ai-studio/how-templates) | You want to design the video yourself from ready-made scenes | A product announcement assembled from scene templates |

<Info>
  **Editable or fast?** Recordings, uploads, presentations, templates, and Prompt a Studio open as projects you can edit scene by scene. Prompt a Motion and Prompt a Motion Premium build a finished animation that you change by typing feedback (on paid plans for Prompt a Motion Premium). For a side-by-side comparison of the prompt options, and for choosing a video type (explainer, how-to, announcement, comparison), see [Prompting best practices](/ai-studio/prompt-a-video-best).
</Info>

## Videos for Reels, Shorts, and TikTok

You can make short videos for social media with **Prompt a Motion Premium**: choose **portrait (9:16)** for Reels, Shorts, and TikTok, or **square (1:1)** for LinkedIn and social feeds. See [Video formats](/ai-studio/pamp#video-formats).

The result is an animated product video in a social format: bold text, motion graphics, your screenshots and logo, voiceover, and music. It is not filmed or photorealistic footage.

<Note>
  Every other option creates a landscape (16:9) video, which works best for websites, YouTube, product pages, help centers, and presentations.
</Note>

## What Demokraft AI can't do

Set your expectations before you start. Demokraft AI does **not**:

- **Generate realistic or cinematic footage.** It can't create real-looking people, places, products, or scenes, and it doesn't make "AI movie" style clips.
- **Create AI avatars or on-camera presenters.** Narration is an AI voiceover, not a person talking on screen.
- **Make lifestyle, vlog, or trend-style reels.** Content built around people on camera, or entertainment with no product to show, is not what it's for.
- **Produce podcasts or webinar videos.** It is not built for recording or editing podcasts, full webinar sessions, interviews, or panel discussions. A short product teaser or recap for a webinar is fine.
- **Work as an editor for camera footage.** You can add a short filmed clip to a project (see below), but it is not designed for editing event recordings, vlogs, or other filmed video.
- **Show your real product screens without screenshots.** Prompt-based videos don't need screenshots: without them, the video tells your story with animated text and motion graphics. To show your actual product, attach screenshots or use a website that has them.
- **Invent facts.** It won't add prices, customer numbers, integrations, or claims that your prompt or website doesn't state.
- **Make long videos from a prompt.** Prompt-based videos are short, up to 90 seconds. For longer videos, record your screen, upload a recording, or convert a presentation.
- **Change a video's format later.** Choose portrait or square when you create a Prompt a Motion Premium video. A landscape project can't be turned into a portrait or square one.
- **Offer hands-on editing for motion videos.** In Prompt a Motion and Prompt a Motion Premium videos, you can't edit the script, music, or scenes directly, and you can't translate them afterward. Request changes through feedback instead.

### If you need realistic or filmed footage

- **Bring your own clips.** Add a video clip you already have:
  - In a recorded, uploaded, or presentation project, add it as a new scene. See [Add, update & delete scenes](/ai-studio/seg-add-del).
  - In a template-based project, add it as an [image or video element](/ai-studio/image-video-element).
- **Work with our production team.** For a brand film, homepage hero video, or paid campaign, our [AI Creative Service](/account/plans-and-pricing#ai-creative-service) produces bespoke videos for you.

## Write a product description, not a shot list

When you use Prompt a Studio, Prompt a Motion, or Prompt a Motion Premium, your prompt is **a description of your product, with context for the video**. Demokraft AI plans the scenes for you.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Won't work" icon="circle-x">
    "Create a 10-second ultra-photorealistic fashion commercial. Use the same model throughout. 85mm lens, softbox lighting, rack focus. 0–1s: full-body hero shot. 1–2s: collar macro. No voice, no text."

    This asks for filmed, realistic footage, a camera plan, and a second-by-second timeline. Demokraft AI doesn't make that kind of video.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Works" icon="circle-check">
    "Product: StyleDesk, a merchandising app for online clothing stores. Audience: e-commerce managers. Purpose: launch our new lookbook builder. Problem: building product pages takes days. Benefits: pages in minutes, consistent branding, faster launches. Call to action: Start a free trial at styledesk.example."

    This describes the product, the audience, and the goal. Demokraft AI writes the story and the storyboard from it.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

You don't need to plan the scenes yourself. Demokraft AI shows you the storyboard for review before it builds the video, and you can ask for changes at that point. For what to include in a prompt, see [Prompting best practices](/ai-studio/prompt-a-video-best).

## Tips for your first video

- **Start with what you already have.** A screen recording or a slide deck gives the most accurate product video, because it shows your real product. A recording isn't polished automatically: see [Record & Upload Tips](/ai-studio/record-upload-best).
- **Set up your brand once.** Save your brand colors, fonts, logo, product name, tagline, call to action, and brand voice in a [Brand Kit](/ai-studio/brand-kit). Attach it when you create a video, and every video uses the same brand theme and language without you repeating it in each prompt or project.
- **Polish in one click with a preset.** A [preset](/ai-studio/preset) saves your usual polish choices (script enhancement, AI voiceover, captions, background, music, intro, and outro) and applies them to a recorded, uploaded, or presentation project in one click.
- **Give the AI real details.** When you start from a prompt or website, add real numbers and benefits. Screenshots help, but they're optional. See [Prompting best practices](/ai-studio/prompt-a-video-best).
- **Keep it short and focused.** One message per video works best. You can make more videos later.
