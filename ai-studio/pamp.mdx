## Overview

Prompt a Motion Premium is a Demokraft feature for creating professional product videos with premium motion graphics, AI voiceovers, music, and sound effects.

<Frame>
  <img src="https://storage.googleapis.com/dk-transcoder-assets/seller/dca5540c-3f1e-494a-950f-ef227365a8d7/studio/0734624d-c100-4c81-adf1-762c4bcad2c6/video_screenshot_file/docv2_0.jpg" alt="Prompt a Motion Premium is a Demokraft feature for creating professional product videos with motion" />
</Frame>

It offers approximately nine styles (more coming soon) for creating on-brand videos that match different communication goals.

You can create a video from a product description or a website URL.

The workflow includes providing product information, configuring video settings, generating and reviewing a storyboard, editing the result in Studio Editor, and exporting the finished video for a product launch, solution introduction, or innovation showcase.

## With Web URL

Prompt a Motion Premium creates product videos from a product description or an existing webpage. Set up the source, style, video settings, audio, and additional assets before selecting **Generate Video**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Prompt a Motion Premium">
    From the home page, select **Prompt a Motion Premium**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a setup tab">
    Choose a setup tab:
    - **Website URL tab**: Create the video from an existing webpage.
    - **Prompt tab**: Describe the product and video purpose without using a website URL.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://storage.googleapis.com/dk-transcoder-assets/seller/dca5540c-3f1e-494a-950f-ef227365a8d7/studio/0734624d-c100-4c81-adf1-762c4bcad2c6/video_screenshot_file/1793104894012.png" alt="Website URL and Prompt setup tabs" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure video settings and generate">
    Configure the video settings, add assets, and select **Generate Video**:
    - Preview the style samples, then select a style. Nine styles are currently available, with more planned for future releases.
    - Select a format: landscape (16:9), portrait (9:16), or square (1:1). See [Video formats](#video-formats).
    - Select the language: English, French, German, Spanish, Portuguese, Hindi, Marathi, Bengali, Gujarati, Punjabi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Japanese, Korean, or Chinese. Two styles (Motif reel and Token morph) support only English, French, German, Spanish, and Portuguese; when you select another language, they are hidden from the style list.
    - Set the duration and video type.
    - Attach a **Brand kit** to apply your brand details throughout the video. See the [Brand Kit guide](https://docs.demokraft.ai/ai-studio/brand-kit).
    - Choose whether to include a voiceover and background music, then select from the available options. Turn off the voiceover to create a music-only video.
    - Use each style's default voice and music selections, or upload your own music file.
    - Add product screenshots or other assets that are not available on the website.
    - If you did not attach a **Brand kit**, upload your logo and any additional assets you want to include.
    - Select **Generate Video** to begin.

## With prompt

The **Prompt tab** lets you create a video without a website URL. Provide the product information, visual assets, and brand settings before continuing.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe your product">
    In the **Prompt tab**, describe your product and the purpose of the video. Use the sample description as a guide.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://storage.googleapis.com/dk-transcoder-assets/seller/dca5540c-3f1e-494a-950f-ef227365a8d7/studio/0734624d-c100-4c81-adf1-762c4bcad2c6/video_screenshot_file/docv2_13.jpg" alt="In the Prompt tab, describe your product and the purpose of the video" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add assets and brand colors">
    Attach relevant images and provide any required assets and brand colors:
    - If a **Brand kit** is selected in the color palette, it supplies the brand details instead. Colors or a font you write in the prompt still take priority.
    - Alternatively, choose from the preset color combinations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply fonts and remaining settings">
    Use the attached **Brand kit** for fonts and other brand details. Set the language and remaining settings from the options available.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Video formats

Every style can create videos in three formats:

| Format | Best for |
|---|---|
| **Landscape (16:9)** | Websites, YouTube, presentations, product pages |
| **Portrait (9:16)** | Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, TikTok, mobile stories |
| **Square (1:1)** | LinkedIn and social feeds |

- Choose the format before you select **Generate Video**. A format a style does not support appears greyed out.
- **Studio Editor** shows a portrait or square video in its own shape, in normal and full-screen view.
- By default, captions sit above the area that social apps cover with their controls on portrait videos, and inside the frame on square videos.
- The export keeps the format. The resolution names the short side: in portrait, 1080p exports at 1080x1920; in square, at 1080x1080.


## Generate and approve the storyboard

Prompt a Motion Premium prepares a storyboard from your product description or Website URL tab. Review the storyboard before the platform builds your video.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Initiate video generation">
    Select **Generate Video** to process the product description or website URL you provided:
    - If you provided a website URL, the platform scans it, retrieves relevant details and images, and prepares a storyboard for review.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the storyboard">
    Review the storyboard and provide feedback to regenerate it, or approve it when you are satisfied.
    - The top of the storyboard confirms the style, format (landscape, portrait, or square), and length you chose.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve the storyboard">
    Approving the storyboard starts video generation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for processing">
    Allow the platform time to complete the process and deliver your video:
    - Storyboard preparation and video generation may take five to ten minutes, depending on the selected duration.
    - Free users can create videos up to 20 seconds long. Paid users can generate videos up to 90 seconds long.
    - Generation time also depends on the selected style and other settings.

## Review and export the video

Once the video is built, it opens in a new tab in **Studio Editor**. Use **Studio Editor** to play back and review the completed video, add captions, revise the draft, and export it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review the playback">
    Open the completed video in **Studio Editor** and play it back to review the result.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://storage.googleapis.com/dk-transcoder-assets/seller/dca5540c-3f1e-494a-950f-ef227365a8d7/studio/0734624d-c100-4c81-adf1-762c4bcad2c6/video_screenshot_file/docv2_23.jpg" alt="Open the completed video in Studio Editor and play it back to review the result" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refine the draft">
    Review and refine the draft in **Studio Editor**:
    - Choose a caption style and apply it to the video.
    - If you are a paid user, provide feedback to revise and refine the draft. This feature is not available to free users.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://storage.googleapis.com/dk-transcoder-assets/seller/dca5540c-3f1e-494a-950f-ef227365a8d7/studio/0734624d-c100-4c81-adf1-762c4bcad2c6/video_screenshot_file/docv2_25.jpg" alt="If you are a paid user, provide feedback to revise and refine the draft" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export the video">
    Select **Export** when you are satisfied with the final video. The export process is the same as for other video features.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://storage.googleapis.com/dk-transcoder-assets/seller/dca5540c-3f1e-494a-950f-ef227365a8d7/studio/0734624d-c100-4c81-adf1-762c4bcad2c6/video_screenshot_file/docv2_26.jpg" alt="Select Export when you are satisfied with the final video" />

    - Depending on your subscription, choose 720p, 1080p, 2K, or 4K. You can also create an animated GIF.
    - Portrait and square videos export in their own shape, GIFs included (see [Video formats](#video-formats)).

</Steps>
